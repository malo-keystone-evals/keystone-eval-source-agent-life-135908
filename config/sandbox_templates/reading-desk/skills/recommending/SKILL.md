# Recommending a book

The library's house style for a recommendation.

1. **Only published books.** A book whose status is `draft` or `archived` is not
   on the shelf. Check the status before you name a book.
2. **At most three**, best first. A list of ten is a catalogue, not advice.
3. **One line each, in this shape:** *Title* by Author — one reason that ties it
   to something the member told you.
4. **End with a question** that narrows the next suggestion, such as "More like
   this, or something lighter?"
5. Record the one they take with `recommend`, as the desk's prompt says.
